Construction

What's actually inside the door

Every JSW Avante door is built the same way, whichever finish sits on top: pre-galvanised steel skins on both faces, a honeycomb core for rigidity without weight, and a welded steel sub-frame that keeps the door square for the life of the building.

  • Face materialGalvanised / Galvalume steel sheet
  • CorePaper-resin honeycomb, edge to edge
  • FrameWelded steel sub-frame
  • CoatingColour-coated per IS 14246-95
